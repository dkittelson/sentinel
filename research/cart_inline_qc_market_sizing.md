- Tecartus (Gilead/Kite)
- Breyanzi (BMS)
- Abecma (BMS)
- Carvykti (J&J/Legend) - ~$1.3B+ annualized run rate

### Combined CAR-T Product Revenue
- 2023: ~$4B+ combined
- 2024: ~$4.5-5B+ combined (Yescarta $1.6B + Carvykti ~$1.3B + Kymriah $443M + others)

### Estimating Manufacturing Lines

**Deriving from production volumes:**
- North America: >25,000 autologous batches/year
- Asia-Pacific: >10,000 patient-specific batches/year
- Europe: Yescarta Amsterdam alone can treat 4,000 patients/year
- **Total estimated global production: 40,000-50,000 batches/year**

**Deriving from facility data:**
- 311 FACT-accredited hospitals in the U.S. (treatment centers, not manufacturing)
- 135 specialized CGT CDMOs worldwide
- Major manufacturers: Novartis (multiple sites), Kite/Gilead (El Segundo, Amsterdam, others), BMS (multiple), Legend/J&J (Raritan NJ, China, Belgium)
- Plus academic medical centers with GMP suites, and emerging point-of-care facilities

| Category | Estimated Lines |

## 14. Comparison: Alternative Startup Ideas TAM

| Idea | TAM (2030) | CAGR | Competition Level | Moat Potential |
|------|-----------|------|-------------------|---------------|
| **CAR-T In-Line QC** | $1.75B (bottom-up) / $3-8B (PAT market) | 14-26% | Low-Medium (few startups) | High (regulatory + validation) |
| AI Developer Tools | $10-26B | 17-27% | Extremely High | Low (commoditizing fast) |
| AI Code Generation | $4.3-12.6B | 24% | Extremely High | Very Low |
| AI Software Dev (broad) | $0.7-15.7B | 42% | Extremely High | Very Low |

### Why CAR-T QC is more attractive than AI dev tools for a startup:

1. **Competition:** AI dev tools market has GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Codeium, Replit, hundreds of VC-funded startups, and Big Tech (Google, Microsoft, Meta) all competing. CAR-T QC has maybe 3-5 direct competitors.

2. **Moat:** In biotech tools, FDA validation + GMP compliance + published validation studies create 3-5 year moats. In AI dev tools, any feature can be copied in weeks.

3. **Pricing power:** Biotech tools sell for $100K-500K per unit with 70%+ gross margins. AI dev tools are in a race to zero ($10-20/month/seat).

4. **Customer stickiness:** Once a PAT system is validated in a GMP process, switching costs are enormous (re-validation = $500K+ and 6-12 months). AI dev tools have near-zero switching costs.

5. **Acquisition outcomes:** Repligen, Sartorius, Danaher, Thermo Fisher actively acquire biotech tools companies at 5-15x revenue. AI dev tools acquisitions are more speculative.

6. **Regulatory tailwinds:** FDA is actively pushing for PAT adoption in cell therapy (ICH Q8/Q9/Q10/Q14 guidelines). No equivalent regulatory tailwind in AI dev tools.

## 15. Financial Model Narrative for VCs

### The Pitch Numbers

> "We're building in-line quality control for CAR-T cell therapy manufacturing. The cell therapy QC market is $2.2B today growing at 26% CAGR. There are ~1,000+ manufacturing lines globally today, expanding to 3,000-5,000 by 2030, each needing $250K in hardware and $100K/year in recurring revenue. Our bottom-up TAM is $1.75B by 2030. With comparable biotech tools companies like 908 Devices reaching $60M revenue and getting acquired for $70M, and Repligen growing from $60M to $640M in a decade, this is a category where focused execution creates massive value."

### Key VC-Ready Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Bottom-up TAM (2030) | $1.75B |
| SAM (2030) | ~$268M |
| SOM (Year 5) | ~$18M ARR |
| Hardware ASP | $250K |
| Annual recurring per line | $100K |
| Gross margin target | 70-80% (hardware + software) |
| Sales cycle | 6-12 months |
| Net dollar retention target | 120%+ (expansion into more lines) |
| Acquisition comps | 5-15x revenue (Repligen, Danaher, Sartorius patterns) |
| Exit scenarios | Strategic acquisition ($100M-500M at $15-50M ARR) or IPO path |

### Manufacturing Cost Context (Why This Matters)
- CAR-T manufacturing cost: $170K-220K per patient batch
- CAR-T therapy price: $373K-475K per patient
- Failed batches are catastrophic: $200K+ wasted per failure
- In-line QC that reduces batch failure rate by even 5-10% saves $10M+/year for a large manufacturer
- **ROI for customers: 5-10x in Year 1 from reduced batch failures alone**
