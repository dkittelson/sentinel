- Internal cost of alt data team: **$1.5M-$2.5M** (cleaning, managing, analyzing)

## 2. How Hedge Funds & Asset Managers Use Geopolitical Risk Data

### Use Cases by Strategy Type

**Global Macro Funds:**
- Primary consumers of geopolitical signals
- Use for FX positioning, rates trading, commodity exposure
- Systematic macro: quantitative models + algorithms for trade execution based on data patterns
- Discretionary macro: human-interpreted geopolitical intelligence for thematic trades
- Global macro hedge funds with longer lockup periods are "more adept at timing geopolitical risks by increasing market exposures"

**Event-Driven Funds:**
- HFRI Event-Driven Index returned **+5.0%** in strong quarters, best since Q1 2021
- Capitalize on volatility spikes from geopolitical shocks
- Monitor events and assess potential impact on asset prices
- Rapid repositioning around conflict escalation/de-escalation

**Quant/Systematic Funds:**
- NLP sentiment analysis on news, social media, web data
- Machine learning for non-linear relationship detection
- Data fusion: blending sentiment, liquidity metrics, alternative economic indicators
- Generative AI for real-time stress testing simulating geopolitical events

**Commodity Traders:**
- Oil: "fastest bridge from geopolitics to inflation expectations"
- Track military activities, policy changes impacting supply routes
- Monitor political developments across energy regions
- Use AI-driven geopolitical intelligence for real-time commodity pricing

### Specific Trading Applications
1. **Commodity price prediction:** Track conflict escalation near supply routes (Strait of Hormuz, Red Sea, Black Sea)
2. **FX hedging:** Geopolitical risk most pronounced for EM currencies; used to time hedges
3. **Country risk scoring:** Systematic scoring for sovereign bond positioning
4. **ESG/Sovereign ESG:** Growing regulatory requirement; cheapest alt data category
5. **Credit risk:** Sovereign risk premiums increase 30bps (advanced) to 45bps (EM) after geopolitical events
6. **Equity positioning:** Average 2.5% stock decline following trading partner military conflict involvement

## 3. Key Buyer Segments

### Quant Funds
- **Market size:** $5.3T hedge fund industry (2024), growing 4.1% CAGR to $7.3T by 2034
- Largest alt data consumers; subscribe to 43+ datasets
- Need structured, machine-readable feeds (APIs, flat files)
- Price-insensitive if alpha-generating; budget $5M+/year for data

### Global Macro Funds
- Core geopolitical risk consumers
- "Geopolitics has risen to the top spot on the list of client priorities for asset managers, from somewhere in the top 20 a decade ago"
- JPMorgan launched dedicated Center for Geopolitics
- Lazard Asset Management launched geopolitical advisory division (2022)
- Goldman Sachs launched geopolitical advisory division (2023)

### Sovereign Wealth Funds
- Nearly all view AI as essential for investment processes
- GIC, Temasek operate dedicated technology teams
- PIF has established innovation hubs
- Heightened geopolitical and sanction risks rank among top concerns
- Need multidisciplinary risk assessment (geopolitics + climate + technology)

### Banks
- Regulatory-driven demand (Basel III, ECB stress testing)
- ECB conducting **first-ever reverse stress test on geopolitical risk**
- Need to identify and quantify how geopolitical events would affect them
- Basel III requires capital adequacy and stress testing for credit, market, operational risks
- Growing demand for scenario analysis data providers

### Commodity Traders
- Global commodity trading revenue hit record levels in recent years
- Higher volatility = shorter cycles due to energy transition, geopolitical fragmentation, climate events
- Need real-time intelligence on supply disruptions, shipping routes, sanctions

### Insurance/Reinsurance
- Political risk insurance market: **$1.24B (2024) growing to $2.20B by 2034** (5.9% CAGR)
- Market capacity: **~$4B in 2025** ($2.23B private + $1.75B Lloyd's)
- **74% of globalized companies** list political risk among top 5 risks
- Demand for political risk cover expected to rise **33%**

## 8. Regulatory Drivers

### ECB Geopolitical Stress Testing (NEW - 2025)
- ECB conducting **first-ever reverse stress test specifically on geopolitical risk**
- Banks must identify and quantify how geopolitical events affect them
- Creates direct demand for geopolitical scenario data providers
- This is a game-changing regulatory tailwind for Sentinel

### Basel III / Basel III Endgame
- Capital adequacy requirements for credit, market, operational risk
- Stress testing must include scenarios of "widening yield spreads in recessions"
- Federal Reserve uses supervisory stress test to set stress capital buffer (SCB)
- Banks need quantitative risk projection and economic capital forecasting

### EU ESG Regulations
- EBA finalized guidelines on ESG risk management (January 2025)
- SFDR, CSRD mandating ESG disclosure including political violence factors
- NIS2 directive increasing demand for proactive intelligence platforms

### Climate + Geopolitical Convergence
- Banks struggling with climate scenario modeling (data gaps)
- Geopolitical disruption to energy transition = combined risk
- Growing demand for integrated climate + geopolitical scenario data
