### Cost per disruption event
- **30%** of supplier disruptions cost organizations **more than $5 million each**
- **16%** of disruptions cost businesses **more than $10 million per event**
- **81%** of respondents have been impacted by supplier disruptions in the past two years

## 4. Industries Most Affected by Conflict-Zone Supply Chain Disruption

### Shipping & Maritime
- Red Sea attacks caused **90% decrease** in container shipping through the region (Dec 2023 - Feb 2024)
- Suez Canal transits fell from **2,068 to 877** ships/month
- Rerouting around Cape of Good Hope adds **11,000 nautical miles**, **10 extra days**, and **$1M+ in fuel per voyage**
- Container rates from Shanghai to US East Coast rose **193%** (Oct 2023 to Feb 2024)
- Shanghai-Europe spot rates up **256%** due to Houthi attacks

### Energy
- Russia-Ukraine conflict caused European energy cost surges
- Reduced Russian supply forced energy supply chain restructuring across Europe
- LNG shipping routes affected by conflict zones

### Agriculture & Food
- Ukraine grain export disruptions caused global food shortages and price hikes
- Cacao prices soared **~300%** in late 2024/early 2025
- Food, beverage, pharmaceuticals, and biofuels all impacted by agricultural commodity volatility

### Manufacturing
- Average lead time for production materials: **79 days** (April 2024), still 21% above 2019 levels
- Production costs raised **~20%** on average for tariff-affected industries
- Global supply chains saw **38% rise in disruptions** in 2024

## 6. Red Sea/Houthi Disruption — Case Study

### Timeline
- **Oct 2023:** Houthi attacks begin on commercial shipping in Red Sea
- **Dec 2023 - Feb 2024:** 90% decrease in container shipping through Red Sea
- **2024-2025:** Ongoing disruption forcing Cape of Good Hope rerouting

### Costs to Industry

| Metric | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Goods disrupted | ~$1 trillion (Oct 2023 - May 2024, Russell Group) |
| Daily cargo blocked | ~$10 billion per day |
| Annual additional trade cost | $15-20 billion (International Transport Forum) |
| Per-voyage rerouting cost | $1M+ in fuel alone |
| Added transit time | +10 days per voyage |
| Added distance | +11,000 nautical miles |
| Container rate increase | 193-256% (Shanghai to US/Europe) |
| Impact on Egypt | $9.4B annual Suez Canal revenue at risk |

### Who Paid the Price
- **Shipping lines:** Absorbed fuel and insurance cost increases
- **Shippers/importers:** Passed-through rate increases of 193-256%
- **Insurance:** War risk premiums spiked dramatically (Maplecroft)
- **Egypt:** Suez Canal revenue ($9.4B/year) severely impacted
- **European consumers:** Higher prices on imported goods
- **Manufacturers:** Extended lead times, inventory disruptions

### What This Proves for Sentinel
The Red Sea crisis is the perfect proof point: companies were caught flat-footed. There was no predictive platform telling supply chain managers "Houthi attack probability is rising, consider rerouting NOW." Insurance premiums spiked reactively. Route changes were made after attacks, not before. A conflict risk intelligence feed integrated into supply chain decision-making would have saved billions.

## 7. Specific Use Cases

### Route Planning & Optimization
- **Problem:** Shipping lines and logistics providers make routing decisions with limited conflict intelligence. The Red Sea crisis showed routes can become unviable overnight.
- **Sentinel value:** Real-time, hex-level conflict probability scores along shipping corridors. API feed: "Bab el-Mandeb strait risk score: 0.87, recommend Cape route."
- **Buyers:** Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, freight forwarders, 3PLs

### Supplier Diversification
- **Problem:** 75% of companies cannot predict supply disruptions. Tier 2/3 suppliers in conflict zones create hidden risk.
- **Sentinel value:** Map supplier locations against predicted conflict zones. Flag suppliers in areas where onset probability is rising.
- **Buyers:** Procurement teams at manufacturers, retailers, pharma

### Inventory Buffering & Demand Planning
- **Problem:** JIT inventory models break when conflict disrupts supply routes. Companies need lead time to build safety stock.
- **Sentinel value:** 7-14 day forward-looking risk scores enable proactive inventory decisions. "Conflict risk in southern Lebanon rising — increase buffer stock for components sourced from Beirut port corridor."
- **Buyers:** Supply chain planners, inventory managers

### Insurance & Parametric Triggers
- **Problem:** War risk premiums are set reactively, spike after events
- **Sentinel value:** Continuous risk scoring enables dynamic, data-driven pricing. Parametric triggers based on conflict probability thresholds.
- **Buyers:** Lloyd's syndicates, marine insurers, parametric insurance providers

### Compliance & Due Diligence (EU CSDDD)
- **Problem:** ~6,000 EU companies must conduct supply chain due diligence by July 2026. Penalties up to 5% of global turnover.
- **Sentinel value:** Conflict risk data as part of human rights due diligence documentation. "This supplier operates in a zone with X% conflict probability."
- **Buyers:** Compliance officers, sustainability teams

## 8. Pain Points with Current Solutions

### What's broken today

1. **Reactive, not predictive.** Current platforms (Resilinc, Everstream) monitor news and events — they tell you what already happened. They don't predict what's coming. Only 25% of firms can predict disruptions.

2. **No granular conflict intelligence.** Existing SCRM tools flag "Middle East instability" as a generic risk. No one provides hex-level, sub-national conflict probability with temporal dynamics.

3. **Visibility gap beyond Tier 1.** Only 6% of businesses have full supply chain transparency. Most companies don't know their Tier 2/3 supplier locations, let alone the conflict risk at those locations.

4. **Siloed systems.** Risk management, procurement, logistics, and compliance teams use different tools with no integration. Cross-departmental coordination is a major weakness.

5. **Slow response times.** By the time a disruption alert fires, rates have already spiked and alternative routes are congested. The Red Sea crisis showed weeks of delay in corporate response.

6. **Generic risk scores.** Competitors offer country-level or region-level risk ratings. Supply chain managers need corridor-level, facility-level precision.

7. **No conflict expertise.** SCRM platforms are built by supply chain people, not conflict analysts. Their geopolitical "risk" is often just news sentiment, not rigorous predictive modeling.

8. **Cost.** Enterprise SCRM platforms cost $100K-$500K+/year. Mid-market companies are priced out but still face conflict-zone exposure.

## 9. Regulatory Drivers

### EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD)
- **Effective:** July 25, 2024 (entered into force)
- **Transposition deadline:** July 26, 2026 (Member States must pass national laws)
- **Scope:** ~6,000 EU companies + non-EU companies with EUR 450M+ EU turnover
- **Requirements:** Identify, prevent, mitigate, and remediate human rights and environmental impacts throughout entire value chain (upstream AND downstream)
- **Penalties:** Up to **5% of global turnover** + civil liability (affected parties can sue)
- **Documentation:** Must retain compliance records for 5+ years
- **Impact on Sentinel:** Companies need data sources to demonstrate due diligence regarding conflict risk in their supply chains. Sentinel's conflict probability data = compliance evidence.

### German Supply Chain Due Diligence Act (LkSG)
- Already in force since January 2023
- Applies to companies with 1,000+ employees
- Similar requirements to CSDDD

### Other Regulatory Tailwinds
- **US Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act (UFLPA):** Forces supply chain transparency
- **EU Deforestation Regulation:** Requires proof of supply chain origin
- **Conflict minerals regulations (EU, US Dodd-Frank Section 1502):** Precedent for conflict-zone supply chain transparency
- **Sanctions compliance (OFAC, EU):** Companies must screen supply chains against sanctioned entities and regions

### Why This Matters
The regulatory environment is creating a **compliance mandate** for supply chain risk data. Companies that previously treated geopolitical risk as optional now face **5% of global turnover penalties** for inadequate due diligence. This converts a "nice to have" into a "must have" — and Sentinel's conflict risk intelligence directly addresses the compliance gap.
