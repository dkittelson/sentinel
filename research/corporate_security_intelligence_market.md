## 2. Corporate Travel Risk Management Market

### Market Size
- **$114.82 billion in 2025**, projected to reach **$188.9B by 2030** at 8.1% CAGR
- Enterprise segment dominates revenue — large corporations have higher travel volumes, higher risk tolerance, and bigger budgets
- North America and Europe retain dominant positions (higher MNC concentration, stronger regulatory environment)

### Key Drivers
- Increasing corporate travel to politically unstable regions
- Heightened security concerns from geopolitical instability, natural disasters, health crises
- Post-COVID recalibration of travel risk policies
- Rising duty of care obligations

### ISO 31030 (Travel Risk Management Standard)
- Released January 15, 2021 by ISO
- Provides framework for Travel Risk Management (TRM) programs
- Not mandatory, but increasingly used as a benchmark
- Covers all aspects of corporate travel — applicable across organizational contexts, travel requirements, and risk appetites
- Creates implicit demand for risk intelligence platforms that can feed TRM programs with real-time data
- **Key implication for Sentinel:** ISO 31030 compliance creates a procurement trigger — organizations need data feeds and risk scoring to implement compliant TRM programs

### Duty of Care Legislation
- Legal/moral obligation for employers to protect traveling employees from hazards and threats
- Growing enforcement globally — companies face litigation risk for inadequate travel risk management
- Good TRM helps companies fulfill legal, moral, and ethical responsibilities
- ISO 31030 adherence demonstrates compliance and reduces liability exposure

- $170.4B spent on exploration in recent 3 years, much in unstable regions (MENA, Sub-Saharan Africa, Latin America)

## 9. How Corporates Assess Conflict Risk

### For Site Selection & Investment Decisions
- **Country Risk Ratings:** Verisk Maplecroft (160+ indices, 198 countries), PRS Group ICRG ratings, Eurasia Group assessments
- **Location-Specific Risk:** Verisk Maplecroft AREA product maps risks to subnational level across 4M+ corporate assets
- **Political Stability Indices:** Government stability, socioeconomic conditions, investment profile, internal/external conflict, corruption, military involvement, religious tensions, ethnic tensions, democratic accountability, bureaucracy quality
- **Scenario Analysis:** Firms like Eurasia Group, Control Risks, and EY provide forward-looking scenario assessments for specific countries/regions
- **Insurance Pricing:** Political risk insurance premiums serve as a market-derived risk signal

### Assessment Framework Components
1. **Governance & Institutions** — regime stability, rule of law, regulatory predictability
2. **Physical Security & Travel** — conflict intensity, crime, terrorism
3. **Economic Risk** — currency stability, sanctions, expropriation risk
4. **Social Risk** — labor unrest, community opposition, human rights exposure
5. **Environmental/Climate Risk** — resource scarcity, climate vulnerability

### Current Gaps (Sentinel Opportunity)
- Most risk platforms provide **country-level** ratings, not subnational/hyperlocal
- Update frequency is typically **quarterly or monthly**, not real-time
- Limited integration of **multi-domain signals** (satellite + connectivity + economic + OSINT)
- Advisory firms charge **$50K–$500K+** for bespoke assessments — no self-serve alternative
- AI/ML-driven **predictive** capabilities are nascent in this market

## 10. FDI Risk Assessment Market

### Political Risk Insurance (PRI) Market
- MIGA issued **$9.5B in new guarantees** in FY2025 (record high, up from $8.2B in FY2024)
- MIGA gross outstanding portfolio: **$36.8B** (17% YoY increase)
- Between 2018–2022, PRI insured ~$150B in projects in developing countries
- Export credit agencies provide 78% of PRI, multilateral institutions 7%, private insurers 15%
- **33% increase in PRI demand expected in 2026** — driven by "gray zone" risks (abrupt regulatory changes, discriminatory practices)

### FDI Risk Assessment Tools
- **ICRG (International Country Risk Guide)** — most widely used academic/institutional political risk measure
- **PRS Group** — political risk ratings provider
- **Verisk Maplecroft** — geospatial risk analytics for investors and MNCs
- **S&P Global** — geopolitical risk intelligence solutions for capital allocation
- **BlackRock Investment Institute** — Geopolitical Risk Dashboard for investment decisions

### AI & Next-Generation Assessment
- Data analytics, ML, and AI being introduced as integral to the next phase of political risk management
- Organizations boosting GRC/ESG software spend by **35%+** over next two years
- Gap: Most FDI risk tools are backward-looking indices, not predictive models
