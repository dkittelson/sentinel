### Valuation Multiples at Exit

Aquaculture tech M&A valuation multiples show wide dispersion:
- **Range:** 1x to 35x EV/Revenue
- **Outliers:** Above 100x EV/EBITDA for data-driven platforms with recurring software revenue
- **Premium for:** Strong IP, software integration, proven efficiency gains, recurring revenue
- **Discount for:** Traditional equipment/hardware-only businesses

### Likely Acquirers
1. **AKVA Group (Norway)** -- Market leader, actively acquiring (Observe Technologies, Maritech, Wavemaster, Egersund Net). Launched Fishtalk 5 cloud platform in Mar 2025.
2. **Innovasea Systems (US)** -- Acquired Aquanetix; launched HydroAI in Apr 2024. Active tech acquirer.
3. **ScaleAQ (Norway)** -- Top 5 market share holder in precision aquaculture.
4. **Vitruvian Partners (PE)** -- Acquired majority stake in Aquabyte (Dec 2025). Looking for platform plays.
5. **Major salmon producers:** Mowi, SalMar, Cermaq, Leroy Seafood -- strategic technology partners with venture-like investment appetite. Mowi runs "Mowi 4.0 Smart Farming" digitalization initiative.
6. **Alphabet/Google** -- Spun out Tidal from X moonshot factory; demonstrates big tech interest in aquaculture AI.

**Market concentration:** AKVA Group, ScaleAQ, Skretting, Innovasea, and Aquabyte collectively command 51-61% of the precision aquaculture market.

## 4. INVESTOR APPETITE

### Is There VC Excitement?

**Mixed signals:**
- **Bearish:** Total aquaculture VC funding dropped 28% in 2024 and is tracking down another 50%+ in 2025. The "future looks bleak" per AgriTech Insights headline. No Series C/D rounds in 2024.
- **Bullish:** Deal count is UP (more VCs making smaller bets = broader interest). RAS funding doubled YoY. Nine dedicated ocean funds raised totaling $1B in 2024. Blue Economy VC hit $2.4B in 2024 -- a 10x increase. VC funding in Bluetech/ocean observation hit $728M in 2025, up 53% from 2024.

**Net assessment:** The sector is in a "flight to quality" phase. Capital is concentrating in proven, scalable companies with clear paths to revenue. Early-stage is harder; growth-stage with revenue is still fundable.

### Blue Economy / Ocean Tech Trends
- Blue Economy is one of the **fastest growing VC sectors** -- nearly 300% increase in 6 years
- But still much smaller than climate tech overall (climate tech attracted 15x more funding in 2024)
- Sustainable ocean economy could deliver **$15T+ in net positive economic benefit** (WEF)
- Blue bond market has **tripled in 5 years** (Bloomberg)
- Aquaculture startups have received ~60% of all "Organics" ocean-climate tech funding since 2014

### ESG / Sustainable Protein Tailwinds
- Atlantic salmon market targeting $26.5B by 2030
- Global aquaculture market projected to reach $370.54B by 2034
- Precision aquaculture market: $848M (2025) to $1.43B (2030), 11.1% CAGR
- Growing regulatory pressure (ESG mandates) driving adoption of monitoring/reporting tech

### Aquaculture-Specific Data Points

- **AquaExchange** (Series B, $8M, Mar 2026): Projects revenue of INR 275 crore (~$33M) for FY26. Technology division revenue grew 6x YoY. Monitors 80,000+ acres globally across 5 Indian states + Ecuador, Saudi Arabia, Madagascar.
- **Aquabyte** ($48.4M total raised before PE acquisition): Revenue not disclosed, but PE interest suggests meaningful recurring revenue.
- **Tidal** (Google X spinout): 250+ pens installed globally -- at estimated $5K-20K/pen/year, implies $1.25M-5M ARR range.

### Path to $100M ARR -- Realistic?

**Challenging but not impossible.** Key considerations:
- Precision aquaculture TAM is ~$1.4B by 2030 -- capturing 7% market share = $100M
- Broader aquaculture tech TAM is much larger ($370B+ total market)
- T2D3 framework (triple 2x, double 3x) suggests 6-9 years from $1M ARR
- Only ~3% of SaaS startups ever reach $100M ARR
- **Most realistic path:** Start with precision aquaculture SaaS, then expand into adjacent verticals (feed optimization, health monitoring, compliance/ESG reporting)
- **Key metric to hit:** 120% net revenue retention (average for $10M-$100M ARR SaaS companies)

**Realistic timeline:** $100M ARR possible by Year 8-10 with multi-product strategy and international expansion. Pure-play aquaculture-only SaaS would struggle to get there; need to be a platform.

## 7. SUMMARY & INVESTMENT THESIS

### The Bull Case
- Aquaculture is the **fastest-growing food production sector globally** ($370B+ market by 2034)
- Precision aquaculture is nascent ($848M in 2025) with strong growth (11%+ CAGR to $1.4B by 2030)
- Blue Economy VC grew 300% in 6 years and hit $2.4B in 2024
- RAS funding doubled YoY in 2024 despite overall decline
- Strategic acquirers (AKVA, Innovasea, Vitruvian) are actively buying, validating exit path
- ESG mandates and sustainable protein demand provide structural tailwinds
- AI/computer vision for aquaculture is where precision ag was 5-7 years ago

### The Bear Case
- Total aquaculture VC funding declining sharply (-28% in 2024, -53% in 2025 YTD)
- No Series C/D rounds disclosed in 2024 -- growth capital is scarce
- IPO window is essentially closed (zero aquaculture or agtech IPOs in 2025)
- Small TAM for pure-play precision aquaculture ($1.4B by 2030) limits upside
- Customer concentration risk (dominated by a few large salmon producers)
- Long sales cycles in aquaculture industry
- Hardware-heavy models are capital intensive and harder to scale

### The Verdict
The market is in a **"trough of disillusionment"** but with real underlying demand growth. The winning strategy for a new entrant would be:

1. **Software-first with hardware moat** -- recurring SaaS revenue with proprietary sensor/data advantage
2. **Land-and-expand in salmon** -- then expand to shrimp, tilapia, and other species
3. **Target strategic partnerships early** -- Mowi, SalMar, or Cermaq as design partners
4. **Position for acquisition by AKVA, Innovasea, or PE roll-up** as primary exit
5. **Raise from aquaculture-specialist VCs** (Aqua-Spark, Ocean 14, Hatch Blue) who understand long sales cycles
